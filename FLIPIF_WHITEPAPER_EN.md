# WHAT IF? by Schallern.io — $FLIPIF Concept Whitepaper

**Version 0.1 · Working draft · 16 October 2026 · Culturetek Pte. Ltd., Singapore**

> What if one question led a community from a coin story to a real game world?

## 1. Abstract

**WHAT IF? by Schallern.io ($FLIPIF)** is a planned community coin on Solana, to be created on Pump.fun. Its job is attention: it tells a story that leads people from the Solana meme-coin space to a real, playable project, the Schallern coin-throw game (https://schallern.io), and to its planned Founder Collection. The coin has **no role in the game at launch**. Whether it ever gets one is a separate decision that would be announced with rules.

As of this version the coin **has not been created**. Everything below is the concept and the launch plan.

## 2. Why a coin story

Most meme coins are a chart with a joke. $FLIPIF is a chart with a destination. The Schallern demo exists, is free, runs on desktop and phone, and has a Founder Collection in preparation. The story gives a coin holder something to do beyond watching a price: play, give feedback, join the Discord, and, if they want, become part of the Founder Collection. For Schallern the coin is a reach engine; for holders it is a story they can retell.

## 3. The story in seven chapters

1. **Start** — what if the question goes viral on Pump.fun and people share the symbol?
2. **$1M** — what if the first milestone lands and coin, community and playable world appear in one clip?
3. **$4M** — what if the story keeps going and brings buyers, conversations and players to the demo?
4. **Founder NFT** — what if a future campaign reserved 500 Founder NFTs for addresses that bought at least 3 SOL of $FLIPIF and held for six months?
5. **Game world** — what if $FLIPIF later had a real, rule-based role in the game?
6. **Exchange moment** — what if community, usage and trading grew enough to pursue a reviewed spot listing?
7. **Looking back** — what if, a year later, people say the story was there from day one?

All chapters are scenarios. The $1M and $4M figures are story milestones, not promised outcomes. No NFT programme, game utility, exchange application or coin launch is active.

## 5. Relationship to Schallern and the Founder Collection

- **Not a payment token.** Schallern's planned real-money mode uses a stablecoin for stakes; $FLIPIF is not planned as a stake or settlement currency.
- **Not a Founder Collectible.** The Founder Collection is a separate series of 10,000 collectibles with its own whitepaper and mint.
- **Possible future bridges** (scenarios only): reserved Founder NFTs for long-term holders; a cosmetic role in the game. Each would be announced with rules, dates and a fairness review; none exists today.

## 6. Reach model

The reach model behind the story is simple and openly assumption-based: holder addresses → share who see the story → share who visit schallern.io → share who mint a Founder Collectible. Example assumptions used on the concept page: 100,000 holder addresses × 50 % story contact × 35 % site visit × 20 % mint × 1.25 collectibles per buyer ≈ 4,375 collectibles. These rates are hypotheses, not measurements; the real campaign will be judged on source-tagged clicks, visits, wallet connections and on-chain mints.

## 7. Historical comparison method

The concept page compares three Pump.fun-origin coins that reached a major spot exchange (FARTCOIN, PNUT, ACT). Method: the earliest tracked rate from CoinLore, the first daily close on the listing exchange (Kraken or Binance) and the highest print in the first 30 days; multiple = later price ÷ early rate. Example: FARTCOIN $1.4645 ÷ $0.0101 = 145×. These are historical observations of other coins, not expectations for $FLIPIF.

## 8. Launch plan

1. **Decision and preparation:** go/no-go and timing by the operator; a dedicated creator wallet (hardware-secured, separate from the Founder Mint treasury); Pump.fun account; Schallern's X account linked.
2. **Token package:** name, ticker, description without promises, 1000 × 1000 px logo, links (what-if page, X, Discord, Telegram), Metaplex-standard metadata.
3. **Launch:** creation on Pump.fun; mint address published on the what-if page and in #official-links; nowhere else.
4. **After graduation:** Jupiter verification (verified.jup.ag), CoinGecko and CoinMarketCap listings, DEX Screener token profile; token searchable in Solana Explorer via the verifiers.
5. **Measurement:** visitors from the coin to the demo are tracked with the `?ref=FLIPIF` link; the story milestones are reported with data, not adjectives.
